# Commercial license for Solum

This is **how to obtain a commercial license for Solum**. It is **not** itself a signed grant, a price list, or legal advice. Rights come from **[LICENSE](../LICENSE)** (BUSL-1.1) and from a **written agreement** with Synaptic Four.

Solum is **not** a Ferrum add-on SKU. A Ferrum commercial license does **not** include Solum.

## What the Additional Use Grant already covers

See `LICENSE`. Typical permitted use: non-commercial research, academic, and educational use, and internal research that is **not** offered as a paid service to third parties.

## When you need a written commercial license

Ask before production institutional deployment for commercial advantage, embedding Solum in a product you sell, hosted/managed service, or redistribution beyond BUSL.

## How to request

Email **[contact@synapticfour.com](mailto:contact@synapticfour.com)** naming **Solum**, organisation, country, on-prem vs hosted, and approximate scale.

There is **no combo SKU** with Ferrum, BRA, or ga4gh-infra.
